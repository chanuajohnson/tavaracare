# Tavara Language & Communication Guardrails

**Status: Always-on. Non-negotiable.** Applies to website copy, chatbot (TAV), onboarding, emails, blog posts, dashboards, social media, caregiver communication, operational messaging, and any AI-generated content for Tavara.

Pair this document with [`TAVARA_WRITING_STYLE.md`](./TAVARA_WRITING_STYLE.md) (anti-AI-tell rules — no em-dashes, banned AI words, sentence rhythm). That doc governs *how* sentences are built. This doc governs *what words and framing* are allowed.

---

## 1. Core Operating Philosophy

Tavara is **emotionally intelligent care coordination.**

Tavara is **not**:
- an agency
- a staffing company
- a gig marketplace
- a cold SaaS dashboard
- "Uber for caregivers"
- corporate healthcare

Tavara exists to: reduce chaos, coordinate care, support transitions, improve continuity, protect dignity, and help families carry less alone.

Every sentence we publish should reinforce this.

## 2. The Emotional Principle

The home is not a "case." It is someone's history, identity, grief, routine, and emotional ecosystem. Care language stays respectful, emotionally aware, and non-judgmental.

## 3. Tone

Calm. Observant. Trustworthy. Operationally competent. Emotionally intelligent. Warm but not sentimental. Clear but not clinical.

## 4. Banned Words → Preferred Replacements

| Avoid | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hire a caregiver | Arrange care · Coordinate care · Build a care team | "Hire" sounds transactional and marketplace-driven |
| Patient | Loved one · Person receiving care · Family member | "Patient" over-medicalises the home (clinical exception OK) |
| Staff | Care team · Caregiver · Support team | Home care is not institutional |
| Case | Household · Family · Care arrangement | "Case" strips humanity |
| Placement | Match · Care arrangement · Care setup | "Placement" sounds institutional |
| Clean-up | Home preparation · Gentle home support · Preparing the space for care | Preserves dignity |
| Hoarding (public) | Overwhelming environments · Homes carrying years of accumulation · Spaces that may need gradual preparation | Reduces shame and defensiveness |
| Burnout pipeline | Unsustainable long-term · Increases caregiver fatigue and turnover | Too casual for premium trust tone |
| Payroll (family-facing) | Caregiver payment coordination · Payment administration support · Care payment records | Avoid employer ambiguity |
| Families engage caregivers directly | Tavara coordinates the care arrangement · Tavara coordinates caregiver matching and care operations | Operational consistency |
| Training oversight | Care standards · Onboarding standards · Coordination standards | Avoid supervisory/employer implications |
| Agency | Care coordination platform (use "not a traditional agency" only when explicitly differentiating) | Primary positioning |
| Client · Customer · User (family-facing) | Family · Household | We are not transactional |
| Worker · Employee | Caregiver · Care professional | We are not employer |

## 5. Preferred Vocabulary

care coordination · continuity of care · operational support · household rhythm · care transition · trusted care · care environment · care team · shared visibility · thoughtful preparation · sustainable care · support systems · emotional pacing · care village · coordinated care · dignity · consistency · stability · readiness · trust-building

## 6. Core Beliefs (subtly visible in copy)

- **Care is emotional, not just operational.** Acknowledge guilt, resistance, overwhelm, grief, pride, fear, exhaustion.
- **Sustainable care requires systems, not heroics.**
- **Trust is built gradually.** Never apply emotional pressure.
- **Homes must remain homes**, not institutions.
- **Caregiving affects the entire household**, not just the person receiving care.
- **Coordination reduces chaos.** That is the product, not caregiver hours.

## 7. Communication Rules

**Do:** explain calmly · normalise overwhelm · acknowledge emotional realities · use observational language · sound operationally experienced · sound culturally aware · speak plainly.

**Do not:** guilt families · overpromise · sound corporate · sound salesy · sound clinical · sound judgmental · sound startup-trendy · use manipulative urgency.

## 8. Social Media

Prioritise: emotional truth · caregiver realities · family dynamics · transition psychology · operational education · calm authority.

Never: viral gimmicks · fear-based marketing · exploitative emotional footage · "before/after home transformation" content.

## 9. Brand Position

Tavara does **not** sell caregiver hours. Tavara sells **continuity, coordination, reduced chaos, emotional support systems, and sustainable care structures.**

This governs the app, onboarding, chatbot, dashboards, blog, caregiver handbook, and every future feature.

## 10. Financial Privacy on Public Surfaces

(Mirrors `mem://constraints/financial-privacy-public-surfaces`. Treated as part of language guardrails — what you say about money is language.)

**Allowed on public pages, blog, marketing, public chatbot replies:**
- Per-hour care tier rates: **Standard $40/hr**, **Full Service $45/hr**, **Premium $50+/hr**
- Subscription tier **names only**: Basic, Active Care, Premium
- Matching & Placement one-time fee: $1,399 (when contextually appropriate)

**Never public — onboarding-private only:**
- Subscription dollar amounts (weekly or monthly)
- Home Preparation dollar amounts (assessment, guided reset, full reset)
- Day 0 figures, deposits, set-up totals
- Household monthly totals or annual projections
- Lifecycle cost projections

**Word choice on public surfaces:** call the per-hour figure the **"care rate"**, never **"wage"**.

These figures are shared privately with families during onboarding conversations, not on marketing pages, blog posts, or unauthenticated chat.

## 11. Enforcement

- **Project memory** carries the non-negotiables in the always-on Core block: `mem://index.md` → `mem://constraints/tavara-language-guardrails`.
- **TAV chatbot** has these rules injected into its system prompt.
- **Blog editor** surfaces a guardrails panel above the body field.
- **Lovable agent** treats violations as it would any other always-on rule: do not produce copy that breaks the table in section 4 or the financial-privacy rules in section 10.

If a draft, prompt, or feature requires breaking one of these rules, stop and ask the user explicitly. Do not silently override.
